# Privacy Policy for DriftNotes

**Effective Date:** 8 September, 2025  
**Last Updated:** 8 September, 2025

## Overview

DriftNotes ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our note-taking application with AI processing capabilities collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any information whatsoever.** All data remains exclusively on your device and never reaches us through servers or any other means.

### Information That Stays Local on Your Device (Never Sent to Us)
The following information is processed and stored entirely on your device. **None of this data is collected by us, sent to our servers, or accessible to us in any way:**

- **Notes Content**: Text content of notes you create within the application
- **User Settings**: Preferences and configuration settings you choose  
- **API Keys**: Optional third-party AI service API keys you provide for enhanced processing capabilities
- **Application Data**: Note metadata (creation dates, processing status), tags, categories, and user preferences

### What We Actually Collect
**Nothing.** We have no servers, no analytics, no data collection of any kind. Your data never leaves your device except when you choose to send notes to third-party AI services for processing.

## How Your Information is Used (All Locally on Your Device)

**Since we don't collect any information, we don't use your information. All processing happens locally on your device.**

### Local Processing (On Your Device Only)
- **Note Analysis**: Your notes are processed locally on your device to extract keywords, categorize content, and determine priority levels
- **Data Organization**: Information is used locally to organize your notes using the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent/important categorization)
- **Search and Discovery**: Content is indexed locally on your device to enable fast search and retrieval

### AI Processing (Your Device to Third-Party AI Services)
- **Third-Party AI Services**: When you choose to use AI features, your note content is sent directly from your device to third-party AI services (such as OpenRouter) for enhanced analysis
- **Default API Keys**: If you don't provide your own API keys, the app uses our default API keys to facilitate this direct connection between your device and the AI services
- **No Intermediary**: We do not receive, process, or store any of the data sent to AI services - it goes directly from your device to the AI provider
- **Processing Scope**: AI processing includes keyword extraction, content categorization, priority assessment, and tag generation

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **Device-Only Storage**: All your notes and personal data are stored locally on your device using SQLite database
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not automatically sync your data to cloud services
- **Data Persistence**: Your data remains on your device until you delete the app or manually delete content

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Sensitive data is protected using industry-standard encryption methods
- **Access Controls**: Only the DriftNotes application can access its data storage
- **Secure Transmission**: When communicating with AI services, data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS)

## Third-Party Services

### AI Processing Services
- **Service Providers**: We use OpenRouter and potentially other AI service providers for note processing
- **Data Sharing**: When using AI processing features, your note content is sent directly from your device to these third-party AI services for analysis
- **Important Note**: These AI services may collect and process your note content according to their own privacy policies
- **Third-Party Policies**: OpenRouter and other AI services have their own privacy policies that govern their use of your data
- **User Control**: You can choose to provide your own API keys for these services or disable AI processing entirely
- **No Server Involvement**: We do not receive, store, or have access to the data sent to AI services - it goes directly from your device to the AI provider

### No Other Third Parties
- We do not share your personal information with advertising networks, analytics companies, or other third parties
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- We have no servers that collect or store your data

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Deletion**: You can delete individual notes or all your data by uninstalling the application
- **API Key Management**: You can add, remove, or modify your third-party API keys at any time
- **Processing Control**: You can choose whether to enable AI processing features

### Data Portability
- **Export**: You may be able to export your notes content (specific export features depend on app capabilities)
- **Local Access**: Since data is stored locally, you maintain direct control over your information

## Data Retention

- **Local Storage Only**: Your notes and data remain exclusively on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app
- **No Server Storage**: We do not have servers that store your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete
- **Third-Party AI Services**: Any data retention by AI services (like OpenRouter) is governed by their own policies - we have no control over or access to this data

## Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## Technical Implementation Details

### Data Types Processed
- **Note Content**: Raw text content of your notes
- **Metadata**: Creation dates, modification dates, processing timestamps
- **Derived Data**: Keywords, tags, priority scores, and categorizations generated by AI processing
- **User Preferences**: Settings and configuration choices

### Processing Activities
- **Keyword Extraction**: AI analysis to identify important terms and concepts
- **Content Categorization**: Automatic tagging and classification of note content  
- **Priority Assessment**: Analysis to determine urgency and importance levels
- **Search Indexing**: Local indexing for fast content retrieval

### Data Flows
1. **Input**: You create or modify notes within the application
2. **Local Storage**: Content is immediately stored in local SQLite database
3. **AI Processing**: Content is sent to AI services for analysis (if enabled)
4. **Result Storage**: AI-generated insights are stored locally alongside original content
5. **User Access**: You can view, search, and organize your enhanced notes
